## About the Service

Sustainable landscaping in Lake Tahoe requires thoughtful design and a deep understanding of high-elevation conditions. At over 6,000 feet, properties face short growing seasons, water restrictions, freeze-thaw cycles, and sensitive environmental regulations.

Our sustainable landscaping approach focuses on creating outdoor spaces that:

* Conserve water

* Reduce long-term maintenance

* Improve soil health

* Protect local ecosystems

* Withstand Tahoe’s harsh winters

We design landscapes that are both environmentally responsible and visually stunning — built specifically for the Lake Tahoe Basin.

## Why Is Sustainable Landscaping Important in Lake Tahoe?

Lake Tahoe is one of the most environmentally protected regions in California. Poor drainage, excessive irrigation, and non-native plant selection can lead to erosion, runoff, and long-term property issues.

Sustainable landscaping helps:

* Reduce water usage during dry summer months

* Prevent soil erosion and runoff

* Improve drainage on sloped properties

* Lower long-term maintenance costs

* Support local wildlife and native plant life

For homeowners and vacation rental owners alike, eco-friendly landscaping is a smart long-term investment.
